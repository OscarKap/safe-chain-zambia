# Emergency Support

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, you do not have to face it alone. Help is available — and asking for it is a brave, important step.

## When To Get Help Right Now

Seek emergency support if:

- You are being hurt or threatened
- You have experienced sexual assault
- You feel you might harm yourself
- A friend is talking about ending their life
- A condom broke and you need PEP (within 72 hours)
- You feel unsafe at home

## What To Do in the Moment

1. If possible, move to a safer place — a neighbour, friend, clinic, school, or shop.
2. Call or message someone you trust.
3. If you have airtime, call the closest clinic or hospital.
4. Use the Safe Chain emergency contacts page for nearby help.

## Important Time-Sensitive Care

After possible HIV exposure or sexual violence, visit a clinic within 72 hours.

A health worker can provide:

- PEP (HIV prevention medicine)
- Emergency contraception
- STI treatment
- Injury care
- Counselling and follow-up

The sooner you go, the more they can help.

## If You Are Supporting Someone Else

- Stay calm and listen
- Believe them
- Do not ask blaming questions
- Help them reach a safe place
- Go with them to seek help if you can
- Take any talk of suicide seriously

Helpful things to say:

- "I'm here for you."
- "This is not your fault."
- "Let's get help together."

## Mental Health Crisis

If you are having thoughts of hurting yourself:

- Tell one person — a friend, parent, teacher, or counsellor
- Move to a place where you feel safer
- Remove anything that could harm you
- Reach out to a clinic, faith leader, or youth organisation
- Stay with someone until the feeling passes

You are not weak. You are not alone. Pain can ease with support.

## Where To Find Help

- Government clinics and hospitals
- Police Victim Support Units
- School counsellors
- Trusted community organisations
- Women's and children's protection services
- Faith leaders you trust

Use the Safe Chain Emergency page for quick-dial contacts and the Find Services map for the nearest clinic.

## You Deserve Safety

Reaching out is the strongest thing you can do.

Help exists. Care exists. You matter.
