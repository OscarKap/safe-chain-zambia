# Youth Rights

Every young person in Zambia has rights — at home, at school, in the community, in healthcare, and online. Knowing your rights helps you protect yourself and support others.

## Your Basic Rights

- The right to life, safety, and dignity
- The right to education
- The right to health and healthcare
- The right to be free from violence and abuse
- The right to privacy and confidentiality
- The right to information about your body and health
- The right to express your opinion
- The right to be heard and believed
- The right to play, rest, and grow

## Your Rights in Health Services

You have the right to:

- Visit a clinic without being shamed
- Ask questions about your body
- Receive accurate health information
- Get tested for HIV or other infections privately
- Choose your contraception method with proper counselling
- Refuse a treatment you do not understand
- Have your information kept confidential

Youth-friendly health services exist. You do not need to be afraid.

## Your Rights at School

- The right to learn in a safe environment
- The right to be free from bullying and harassment
- The right to be treated equally regardless of gender or disability
- The right to attend school during your period
- The right to report a teacher or student who harms you

## Your Rights Online

- The right to privacy
- The right to refuse sharing personal photos
- The right to block, mute, or report anyone who threatens you
- The right to ask someone to delete a photo or message about you
- The right to seek help if you are bullied or harassed online

## Rights for Young People with Disabilities

Disability does not reduce your rights. You have the same right to:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Safety
- Information in accessible formats
- Participate fully in community life

## When Your Rights Are Violated

If someone harms or threatens you, you can:

- Tell a trusted adult
- Speak to a teacher, counsellor, or health worker
- Report to the Police Victim Support Unit
- Contact a child or youth protection organisation
- Use Safe Chain's confidential report feature

## Quick Reminder

Your voice matters. Your safety matters. Your future matters.

Knowing your rights is the first step to protecting them.
