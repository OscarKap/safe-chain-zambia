# Healthy Relationships

A healthy relationship — whether with a partner, friend, or family member — is built on respect, safety, trust, and equality. You should feel free to be yourself, speak openly, and grow without fear.

Love should never feel like fear.

## Signs of a Healthy Relationship

- You feel safe and respected
- You can disagree without being punished
- Your boundaries are taken seriously
- You both make decisions together
- You feel free to spend time with friends and family
- You can say no without guilt
- You support each other's goals

## Signs of an Unhealthy Relationship

- Constant jealousy or controlling behaviour
- Checking your phone, location, or messages
- Insults, threats, or humiliation
- Pressure to do sexual things you don't want
- Isolating you from people who care about you
- Using money, gifts, or status to control you
- Hitting, shoving, or any physical harm

If any of these are happening to you, you deserve support. Talk to a trusted adult, counsellor, health worker, or visit the Emergency page in this app.

## Communication That Builds Trust

- Listen without interrupting
- Speak honestly about how you feel
- Use "I feel…" instead of blaming
- Take a pause if emotions get too high
- Apologise when you are wrong — and mean it

## Boundaries Are Healthy

Setting boundaries is not rude. It is one of the most caring things you can do for yourself and the people around you.

Examples of healthy boundaries:

- "I'm not ready for that yet."
- "Please don't share my photos."
- "I need some quiet time tonight."
- "I don't want to talk about that."

A person who truly cares about you will respect your boundaries without punishment.

## Quick Facts

- Healthy love feels calm, not anxious
- Respect is not optional — it is the foundation
- You can leave any relationship that hurts you
- Asking for help is a sign of strength

## Where to Get Help

If you are unsure whether your relationship is healthy, talk to:

- A trusted friend, parent, or guardian
- A school counsellor or teacher
- A youth-friendly health worker
- A community organisation that supports young people
- The Safe Chain emergency contacts page
